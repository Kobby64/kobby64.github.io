## Overview

LION-CATT (Live-streaming Offender Network-based Chat Analysis Triage Tool) is an artificial intelligence-based digital forensics platform designed to assist law enforcement agencies in detecting, capturing, and analyzing real-time data from live-streamed child sexual abuse investigations.

The project focuses on supporting investigators through automated triage, evidence collection, and analysis of online communications associated with child exploitation crimes.

## Research Objectives

* Develop AI-assisted tools for digital forensic investigations
* Support law enforcement in identifying online child exploitation activity
* Automate triage and evidence collection processes
* Improve real-time monitoring and analysis capabilities
